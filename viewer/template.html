How to read it

The left-right position is the detector from your hand-worked sheet: average one group, average the other, draw the line between them. Each clip is scored by a detector built without its key, so a split here is real hearing, not memory.

Up and down carries no measurement. Each row is simply one clip: the top group first, the other below, each sorted from low notes to high. The bar from the centre line shows how far, and which way, the detector scored that clip.

Scores are spread so the cutoff sits at the centre line. Major vs minor shows the wrong-way lean: many top-group bars point the wrong way.
