How to read it

The left-right position is the detector from your hand-worked sheet: average one group, average the other, draw the line between them. Each clip is scored by a detector built without its key, so a split here is real hearing, not memory.

The up-down position is not told about the labels. It shows whatever varies most in the model's numbers, often pitch. If the colours separate up and down but not left and right, the model hears something, but not the thing we asked about.

Scores are spread so the cutoff sits at the centre line. Major vs minor shows the wrong-way lean: the colours cross over the line instead of splitting along it.
